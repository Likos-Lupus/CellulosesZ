# /balance

Show your balance, or another account's balance.

## Behavior

With no argument, shows your own balance. With a name, shows that account's balance if you hold the
required permission. Unknown accounts report a balance of zero without creating a new account.

## Parameters

- `name`: optional account name.

## Example

- `/balance`
- `/balance Alex`

## Related

- `/pay`, `/balancetop`, `/transactions`
