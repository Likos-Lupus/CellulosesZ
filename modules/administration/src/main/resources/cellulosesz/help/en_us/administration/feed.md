# /feed

Restore a player's hunger and saturation.

## Behavior

Refills the named online player's hunger and saturation. With no name, target yourself. Nothing here
is a sanction and no record is stored.

## Parameters

- `player`: optional target; defaults to yourself.

## Example

- `/feed`
- `/feed Alice`

## Failures

- The player is not online.

## Related commands

- `/heal`, `/fly`, `/god`
