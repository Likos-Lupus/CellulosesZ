# /break

Break the block you are looking at.

## Behavior

Breaks the targeted block without drops, matching an administrative removal rather than mining.
Bedrock needs `cellulosesz.break.bedrock`; the reach is 20 blocks.

## Parameters

- None.

## Example

- `/break`

## Failures

- You are not looking at a block within 20 blocks.
- The block is bedrock and you lack `cellulosesz.break.bedrock`.

## Related commands

- `/ice`, `/ext`, `/tree`
