# /kittycannon

Fire a kitten from wherever you are looking.

## Behavior

Launches a baby cat with a short burst of velocity in the direction you are facing. The whole fun
group is disabled with `[administration.fun].enabled = false`.

## Parameters

- None.

## Example

- `/kittycannon`

## Failures

- The fun commands are disabled.

## Related commands

- `/beezooka`, `/antioch`
