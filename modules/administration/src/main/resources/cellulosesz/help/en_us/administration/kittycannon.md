# /kittycannon

Fire a kitten from wherever you are looking.

## Behavior

Launches a baby cat at twice the normal projectile speed in the direction you are facing. One second
later the cat is removed and a zero-power, non-destructive explosion is shown at its position. The
whole fun group is disabled with `[administration.fun].enabled = false`.

## Parameters

- None.

## Example

- `/kittycannon`

## Failures

- The fun commands are disabled.

## Related commands

- `/beezooka`, `/antioch`
