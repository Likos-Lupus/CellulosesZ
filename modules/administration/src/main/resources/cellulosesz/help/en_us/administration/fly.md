# /fly

Toggle flight for a player.

## Behavior

Enables flight if the player can't currently fly, otherwise disables it. With no name, target
yourself. Nothing here is a sanction and no moderation record is stored.

## Parameters

- `player`: optional target; defaults to yourself.

## Example

- `/fly`
- `/fly Alice`

## Failures

- The player is not online.

## Related commands

- `/heal`, `/feed`, `/god`
