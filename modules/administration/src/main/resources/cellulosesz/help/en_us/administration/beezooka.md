# /beezooka

Fire a bee from wherever you are looking.

## Behavior

Launches a bee with a short burst of velocity in the direction you are facing. The whole fun group
is disabled with `[administration.fun].enabled = false`.

## Parameters

- None.

## Example

- `/beezooka`

## Failures

- The fun commands are disabled.

## Related commands

- `/kittycannon`, `/antioch`
