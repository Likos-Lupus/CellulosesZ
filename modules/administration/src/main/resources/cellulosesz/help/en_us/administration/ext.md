# /ext

Extinguish fire.

## Behavior

Clears the target player's remaining fire ticks. It does not remove fire blocks in the world.

## Parameters

- `player`: optional target; defaults to yourself. Requires `cellulosesz.ext.others`.

## Example

- `/ext`
- `/ext Alice`

## Failures

- The target is not online.

## Related commands

- `/break`, `/ice`, `/burn`
