# /ext

Extinguish fire.

## Behavior

Puts out the fire on the target and clears fire blocks in a small radius around them. Without a
target the command acts on yourself.

## Parameters

- `player`: optional target; defaults to yourself.

## Example

- `/ext`
- `/ext Alice`

## Failures

- The target is not online.

## Related commands

- `/break`, `/ice`, `/burn`
