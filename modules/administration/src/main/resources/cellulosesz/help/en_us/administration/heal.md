# /heal

Restore a player's health.

## Behavior

Sets the named online player's health to the maximum. With no name, target yourself. Nothing here is
a sanction and no record is stored.

## Parameters

- `player`: optional target; defaults to yourself.

## Example

- `/heal`
- `/heal Alice`

## Failures

- The player is not online.

## Related commands

- `/feed`, `/fly`, `/god`
