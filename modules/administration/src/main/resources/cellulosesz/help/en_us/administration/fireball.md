# /fireball

Launch a fireball.

## Behavior

Fires a projectile in the direction you are looking. The optional type selects `small`, `large`
(the default), `dragon` or `wither`. The optional speed scales the launch velocity.

## Parameters

- `type`: optional `small`, `large`, `dragon` or `wither`.
- `speed`: optional velocity multiplier, 0.1 to 10.

## Example

- `/fireball`
- `/fireball small`
- `/fireball large 2.5`

## Failures

- The type is unrecognized.

## Related commands

- `/firework`, `/nuke`, `/lightning`
