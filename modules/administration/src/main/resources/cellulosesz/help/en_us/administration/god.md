# /god

Toggle invulnerability for a player.

## Behavior

Enables damage immunity if the player isn't invulnerable, otherwise disables it. With no name,
target yourself. Nothing here is a sanction and no moderation record is stored.

## Parameters

- `player`: optional target; defaults to yourself.

## Example

- `/god`
- `/god Alice`

## Failures

- The player is not online.

## Related commands

- `/heal`, `/feed`, `/fly`
