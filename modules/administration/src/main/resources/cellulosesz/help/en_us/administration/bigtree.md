# /bigtree

Grow a large tree at the block you are looking at.

## Behavior

Like `/tree`, but every kind is the large variant. The supported kinds are `tree` (big oak),
`redwood` (mega spruce), `jungle` (mega jungle) and `darkoak`.

## Parameters

- `type`: one of the supported large tree kinds.

## Example

- `/bigtree tree`
- `/bigtree darkoak`
- `/bigtree jungle`

## Failures

- The tree type is unrecognized.
- You are not looking at a block, or the tree cannot grow there.

## Related commands

- `/tree`, `/break`, `/ice`
