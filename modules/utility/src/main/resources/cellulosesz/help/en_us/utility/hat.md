# /hat

Wear the item in your hand on your head.

## Behavior

Swaps the held item with whatever is on your head. By default only items with an equipment
capability (for example armor or a helmet) can be worn; the server can allow any item.

## Example

- `/hat`

## Failures

- You're not holding an item.
- The item can't be worn as a hat on this server.

## Related commands

- `/itemname`, `/itemlore`
